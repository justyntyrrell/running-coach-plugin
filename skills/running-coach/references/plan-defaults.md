# Plan outline defaults

Rendered from the coaching knowledge (version acf61b6f40f3); never edited by hand.

Each value the plan outline's `why` names as `default:<key>`, with its basis.

- **cold_start_step:** 0.05 (design inference). The weekly step in km, as a share of the recent level, until the Athlete's own past increases replace it. The safety envelope clamps it.
- **band_step:** likely 0.5, stretch 1.0, out_of_reach 0.0, unknown 0.5 (design inference). The share of the step each ambition band takes: a likely goal takes half a step, a stretch a full one, an out-of-reach goal holds, and without On-track half a step.
- **return_step:** 0.1 (design inference). The weekly step in km, as a share of the recent level, while the level is below the return ceiling: an Athlete coming back to a volume they held recently takes it whole, not as the band's share. Runner cohorts flag no weekly rise under about 20%, and a 10% cap was no safer than faster progression (load-ten-percent-rule-not-protective); no study shows a return is safer than a first build. The safety envelope clamps it.
- **return_ceiling:** 0.9 (design inference). The return step stops at the return ceiling: the level, and return_credit's share of the way from it to this share of demonstrated km (the highest 4-week median of the last capacity_weeks). Above it the usual step applies, so a return never goes straight back to the peak.
- **return_credit:** capacity_weeks 26, kept_share 0.667, stopped_share 0.333, half_after 3, gone_after 8 (load-detraining-front-loaded-after-stop). How much of demonstrated km still counts, on a clock of the weeks since it, by their km and not the calendar: a week at kept_share of it or more costs nothing (load-reduced-training-keeps-endurance), one under stopped_share, or without a run, is a stopped week, and one between is half of one. Half the credit is gone after half_after stopped weeks and all of it after gone_after, as a full stop's losses come early and level off by about 8 weeks. Pain or illness in the last 7 days holds the return step.
- **odds:** likely_from 0.667, out_of_reach_below 0.2 (design inference). On-track's odds of the goal time that make it likely (2 in 3 or better) and out of reach (under 1 in 5); a stretch between.
- **recovery_week:** every 4, share 0.75 (design inference). Every 4th week, counted back from the taper (or from the first run's week without a goal), is lighter, at about 75% of the recent level.
- **long_run_growth:** 0.08 (design inference). The long run grows by this share of the longest run of the last 4 weeks, inside long-run-spike's more-than-10% edge.
- **long_run_share:** 0.5 (design inference). The long run's largest share of the week's km, so a week of little volume is never one long run.
- **easy_day_min_km:** 3.0 (design inference). The shortest easy day a laid-out week holds: below it the week has an easy day fewer. No upper edge: a day more than the Athlete runs is their choice, and the long run keeps its own limits.
- **long_run_ceiling:** 5K minutes 90, 10K minutes 90, half_marathon minutes 120, marathon km 35, minutes 180, general minutes 90 (design inference). The longest long run the suggestion grows to, by goal distance (general without a goal): a time at the Athlete's own easy pace of the last 28 days, and for the marathon also a distance, whichever is shorter.
- **marathon_long_runs:** km 32, count 3 (longrun-marathon-training-dose). More runs of 32 km or longer went with faster marathons; the count of 3 reads that as several, a design inference. The projection's shortfall is measured against it.
- **taper_depths:** [0.75, 0.5] (taper-elite-distance-runner-practice). Each taper week's km as a share of the level before the taper, from its first week: elite marathoners cut continuous running to about 71% and then 53%.
- **hard_sessions:** base 1, develop 2, sharpen 2, taper 1, post_race 0 (design inference). Hard sessions a week by stage, before the safety envelope's caps.
- **block_days:** 84 (design inference). The Block, the twelve weeks that lead into a race: before it the stage is base.
- **no_goal_weeks:** 4 (design inference). How far the projection runs without a goal race.
- **km_band:** 0.1 (design inference). A suggested week's km band, this share either side of its target, never past the envelope's soft bound.
- **edge_margin:** share 0.03, evidence_min 3 (design inference). With the Athlete's coach_plans switch on, a week that grows aims at its soft bound less this share, so a small miss in the run-to-run layout stays inside it; once the Athlete has evidence_min increases of their own that no pain followed, the largest of them holds it, and until then cold_start_step does.
- **personal_evidence:** weeks 8, step_min 4, usual_day_share 0.5 (design inference). The Athlete's own record replaces a default once the training log holds this many completed weeks: the step takes at least step_min of their own increases no pain followed, and a usual long-run or hard day is one used in at least this share of those weeks.
- **pain_or_illness_caps:** hard_sessions 0, week_km_over_previous 1.0 (design inference). Pain or illness in the last 7 days holds the week with no quality: the coach's pain rule, not a claim.
- **threshold_vo2_fraction:** 0.88 (design inference). Threshold (T) pace from a race is the speed whose oxygen cost is this share of the race's VDOT, by Daniels & Gilbert's equations: after Daniels, whose T is the speed at 88% of VDOT, and which reproduces his table's 4:15/km at VDOT 50. The training log's race anchor reads it as THRESHOLD_VO2_FRACTION.
- **elevation_cost:** 0.02 (design inference). The Training paces move by this share of pace per 1,000 m between where a session or race was run and the Athlete's home elevation, unless their own altitude fit is supported. Set below the fall in VO2max with altitude, because threshold falls less than VO2max. Wehrlin & Hallén (2006) is its origin, unfetched, so not a claim: it becomes one only in knowledge/claims/, checked by tools/verify_citations.py.
- **threshold_range:** [-0.01, 0.02] (design inference). The Training paces' T range, as shares of the target pace: from 1% faster to 2% slower, leaning slow where the Anchor leans fast.
- **threshold_sessions:** days 42, block_minutes [5, 20], work_minutes 20, recovery_minutes 3 (design inference). The Training paces' sessions candidate: the median grade-adjusted pace of the work blocks of the last 42 days that are 5 to 20 minutes long, in a run of them with recoveries of 3 minutes or less that holds 20 minutes of work. The session's shape admits it, never its Label, so no past Anchor biases it.
- **return_to_quality:** race_days 21, no_hard_days 10, hard_sessions 2 (design inference). An Athlete is coming back to quality while their last race is 21 days old or less, or after 10 days or more without a hard Label (a race is one), until they have run 2 hard sessions since: the Training paces then give T and I as the slow half of their range, so the first sessions back rebuild rather than test.
- **lthr_race_margin:** bpm 4, race_minutes [45, 90] (design inference). A race of 45 to 90 minutes averages within a few bpm of LTHR, so an LTHR setting more than 4 bpm above the newest one's average heart rate looks high: the Training paces' heart-rate bands and ceiling are built on that race's average instead, and the outline flags lthr_above_race_hr. Labels keep the setting.
- **search:** run_km 60, week_km 300 (design inference). The bracket the bounds are bisected in; a bound with no limit inside it is published as null.
- **session_shapes:** kinds easy week_kinds ["easy"], work_bands ["E"], warmup_minutes null, cooldown_minutes null, max_work_share null, recovery week_kinds ["easy"], work_bands ["E"], warmup_minutes null, cooldown_minutes null, max_work_share null, long week_kinds ["long"], work_bands ["E", "M"], warmup_minutes null, cooldown_minutes null, max_work_share null, strides week_kinds ["easy"], work_bands ["R"], warmup_minutes 10, cooldown_minutes null, max_work_share null, marathon_pace week_kinds ["easy", "long"], work_bands ["M"], warmup_minutes 10, cooldown_minutes 5, max_work_share 0.2, threshold week_kinds ["hard"], work_bands ["T"], warmup_minutes 15, cooldown_minutes 10, max_work_share 0.1, intervals week_kinds ["hard"], work_bands ["I"], warmup_minutes 15, cooldown_minutes 10, max_work_share 0.08, repetition week_kinds ["hard"], work_bands ["R"], warmup_minutes 15, cooldown_minutes 10, max_work_share 0.05, race_specific week_kinds ["hard"], work_bands ["goal"], warmup_minutes 15, cooldown_minutes 10, max_work_share 0.15, goal_range [-0.02, 0.02] (design inference). The sessions the coach composes days from, which check_week holds each step to: the week kinds a session goes on, the Training-pace bands its work runs in (goal: the race_specific pace, goal_range either side), the shortest warm-up and cooldown in minutes, run easy (null: a continuous run with none), and its work's largest share of the week's km. The shares follow Daniels' Running Formula (M 20%, T 10%, I 8%, R 5% of weekly mileage); race-specific's 15% and the minutes are design inference. Warm-ups, recoveries between reps and cooldowns run easy or slower.
