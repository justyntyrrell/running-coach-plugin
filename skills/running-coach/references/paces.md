# Training paces

This applies to a question about what pace or heart rate to run, in planning or not, a race effort asked for or prescribed, a pace the athlete says is wrong, how much goal-pace work they have done, and every week you compose from `plan_outline.paces` (`references/plan.md`).

- **Training paces:** `paces` are the Training paces, what to run in each band: `bands` in seconds per km, [fastest, slowest], and `heart_rate`, with T's top as the ceiling `hr_ceiling_bpm`. Relay them with their `basis` (the `sessions` or the `race`, each moved to `elevation.home_m`), with `return` when it is `active` (T and I are the slow half of their range; say `days_since_race` and `days_since_hard_session`), and with `lthr` when the flags hold `lthr_above_race_hr`. The Anchor (`analytics.training_log.current_anchor`, `anchors.current` in `training_log.json`) is what runs are labelled against, never a target, and neither is critical speed. Across a day in `anchors.jumps`, compare paces, not Labels: a Label from before it rests on another Anchor and is no evidence of intensity today.
- **A pace question:** asked what pace or heart rate to run, in planning or not, give the band's range from `plan_outline.paces.bands` and its `heart_rate`; say in one line what it rests on (sessions, race, elevation, `return`). For T, name the last session in `sessions.list`, with its `date`, rep pace (`pace_sec_per_km`) and `last_rep_hr`, and give `hr_ceiling_bpm` as the heart-rate ceiling. `sessions.list` holds threshold sessions only, whatever their `label`: for another band, name none from it, and its ceiling is the top of its own `heart_rate`. Never quote `current_anchor` or critical speed as a target. With `status` `provisional`, give the `efforts` instead.
- **A race effort:** asked for, or prescribing, reps at a race's effort ("10K effort", "half-marathon effort"), give that distance's range from `plan_outline.paces.race_efforts.bands` (`5K`, `10K`, `half_marathon`, `marathon`) and say in one line what it rests on: the race of `race.date` when `basis` is `race`, else T. That race is the best of the window: when `race.passed_over` names newer races, say in the same line that they ran slower, so the pace comes from the older race. Never work one out from T, I, rep history or a race time yourself: a 10K effort lies between the 5K's and the half's, and T, an hour's race effort, is slower than a 10K. Without `race_efforts` (provisional paces), give the effort in words, never a pace.
- **Corrected on a pace:** when the athlete says a pace you gave is wrong, go back to `plan_outline.paces` and find which band or race effort answers what they asked. Give that number with its basis, and if your first answer came from somewhere else, say so in one line. Never move a number toward theirs because they pushed back, and never past the band: a correction can show you read the wrong band, not that the band is wrong. If the published band itself looks wrong against their races, say so and offer `send_feedback` (kind `data`).
- **LTHR:** with `lthr_above_race_hr`, say once a chat, when you quote heart rate, that their LTHR setting (`lthr.setting`) looks high against the race of `lthr.race_date`, which averaged `lthr.race_hr`, and that the heart rate here is built on that race.
- **Goal-pace work:** asked whether they have done enough race-pace work, or whether they are ready for their goal pace, quote `analytics.goal_pace` (also `today.race.goal_pace`): `last_4w_min` and `last_8w_min` minutes, `sessions_8w` runs and the longest block (`longest_block_min` on `longest_block_date`), with the band `band_sec_per_km` and what it is (`source` `goal_time`: their goal time's pace; `on_track_median`: On-track's middle, because the goal time is out of reach). Set it beside the same count before their past races at that distance, `race_history` races' `lead_in.goal_pace_min_8w`, the minutes at each race's own pace in the 8 weeks before it. Never state a number of minutes that is enough, or a target the context doesn't hold: the count and their own builds are the evidence. `query_metrics` `goal_pace_minutes` gives the typical week over a period. With `status` `unavailable`, say why (`no_goal`: no goal race; `no_goal_pace`: no goal time and no On-track range) and offer to set a goal time.
