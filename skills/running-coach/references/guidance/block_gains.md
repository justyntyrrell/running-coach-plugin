# What a dedicated training block is worth, by training status

Rendered from the coaching knowledge (version a596fb349f63); never edited by hand.

## Claims

Each with its evidence type, quality, source and link, then what it means in practice.

- **block-bacon-length-of-responders** (meta_analysis, low quality; Bacon 2013, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24066036/): The gain grows with block length to about 10 weeks: the ramp reaches the full band at 10 weeks.
- **block-bangsbo-trained-55km** (rct, low quality; Bangsbo 2009, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19797693/): A trained runner's block is worth about 2-3%: the top of the trained band. More volume is not the gain.
- **block-festa-recreational-8wk** (rct, low quality; Festa 2019, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33344993/): An 8-week recreational block is worth about 3%, inside the recreational band.
- **block-filipas-well-trained-16wk** (rct, moderate quality; Filipas 2022, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34792817/): The well-trained band: a full block is worth about 0.5-1.5%, below one race's noise.
- **block-haugen-world-class-progression** (observational, low quality; Haugen 2018, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29543080/): Near the ceiling a block is worth 0-1%: a runner whose Rested has not moved in 2 years is in that band.
- **block-lock-short-block-smaller** (meta_analysis, low quality; Lock 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37676620/): A 4-week block is worth only part of a full one: the ramp's 30% at 4 weeks.
- **block-munoz-recreational-10k** (rct, moderate quality; Muñoz 2014, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23752040/): A recreational runner's full block is worth a few percent off a 10K race: the middle of the recreational band.
- **block-munoz-trained-peak-6wk** (rct, low quality; Munoz 2015, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26448854/): A trained runner's peaking block is worth under 2%: the bottom of the trained band.
- **block-nuuttila-recreational-12wk** (rct, moderate quality; Nuuttila 2022, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35975912/): The recreational band spans these two arms: about 3% for a sensible fixed block, up to 6% when it goes well.
- **block-rosenblat-status-gradient** (meta_analysis, moderate quality; Rosenblat 2021, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33826121/): The status gradient scenario 3 rests on: the less trained the runner, the larger the share a block can take off a time trial. It anchors the novice band's 6% and the trained band's middle.
- **block-vesterinen-recreational-8wk** (rct, low quality; Vesterinen 2016, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26909534/): The bottom of the recreational band: a short intensive block can be worth as little as 1-2%.
- **block-wiesinger-elite-hiit** (meta_analysis, low quality; Wiesinger 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39830026/): Near the ceiling the gain is uncertain and small: the near-ceiling band's 0-1%, and no gain past 16 weeks.
