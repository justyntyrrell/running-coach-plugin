# Time trial

A time trial is a maximal effort you prescribe so that its result counts as a race. The hub offers one to a cold-start athlete with a goal race, once per goal race, as the agenda's `time_trial` item: propose one in this chat, after what the athlete came for and anything urgent, and report it with `agenda_done`. Say why it comes now, from its `data.trigger`: no run of theirs looks like a race (`no_race_candidates`), or the race question has been put in two weeks without a race (`race_asks`); `data.retry` is true for the retry after a miss. When the agenda also has a `race_question` item, ask its runs first, and propose the time trial only if they confirmed none as a race: one confirmed race may end their cold start without a hard session. Without the item, never start one on your own: only a retry or the athlete asking brings one (below). Every time trial you propose follows these rules.

- **What kind:** a 5K for a 5K, 10K or half marathon goal, and a 10K for a marathon goal (for another distance, a 5K up to 25 km and a 10K beyond): the race prediction prices no distance more than five times its nearest race. Run as a parkrun, or a timed road race of that distance, where the athlete can get to one; otherwise solo, on a flat road route with no stops. It is never on a treadmill or trail, which the race prediction drops, never shorter than 5K and never a half marathon or longer, which costs about a week of training.
- **Where:** in the first week, after their yes, that still has a quality session ahead, in place of that session; a warm-up and cool-down keep the week's running distance as usual, so it is raced in normal training, not after a taper. It is never in the 21 days before the goal race, where it could change the quoted prediction during the taper, and not with pain or illness in the last 7 days (rule 3; ask for a check-in before it, as before any key workout, `references/check-ins.md`). When no such week comes before the 21 days, there is no time trial: say so. Like any calendar change, propose it under rule 6, showing the session and the time trial in its place, and write it only after their yes. Name the session it replaces in the `Coach change` line's reason (for example `Coach change 2026-10-20: 5K time trial in place of 6 x 800 m`) and, with `save_coach_memory`, save it as a decision with its date and the session it replaces, so a later chat can put that session back.
- **If cold start ends first:** when a race they confirmed ends their cold start before the day (`analytics.race_prediction`'s `basis.races_used` is above 0), no time trial is needed: propose putting the original session back, under rule 6.
- **Counting it:** in the first chat after the run, ask one question about that run, by its date: was it all-out from start to finish? You may quote the RPE they logged on it (`rpe` in `list_recent_activities`) when asking, but never save an answer from RPE, heart rate or pace: only their word counts. Save `race` or `not_race` with `confirm_races` as `references/race-questions.md` says, with the run's `activity_id` and date from `list_recent_activities` or `training_log.json`. Ticking Race on it in Intervals counts too. A race counts from the next sync. When that sync still has no race (`basis.races_used` is 0) and `analytics.race_prediction`'s `basis.races_excluded` names a code, the race prediction dropped the run: name it (for example `below_race_heart_rate` or `too_hilly`), say they are still cold-start, and treat it as a miss.
- **A miss:** a time trial that was not all-out, dropped by the race prediction, cut short or not run. Propose one retry, in the chat where the miss shows up: at least 2 weeks after it and still outside the 21 days, placed and proposed as above, naming the race prediction's reason when it dropped the run, so the retry avoids it. With `save_coach_memory`, save each miss as a decision, so a later chat knows whether the retry is used. After a second miss, propose none unless the athlete asks for one or a new goal race brings the `time_trial` item.
- **A decline:** a no to the proposal. Do not press. Keep quoting what `analytics.race_prediction` gives without a race: the range from their training alone. Say once what that leaves out: a race of their own, which the time trial would have added. With `save_coach_memory`, save the decline as a decision, and propose none again unless they ask for a race time or a new goal race brings the `time_trial` item. Keep asking the race question as `references/race-questions.md` says.
