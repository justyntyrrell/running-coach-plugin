# Pace Puffin

Coaching from your own runs.

Pace Puffin is a running coach that works from your own training history. It reads the runs your watch recorded, and the heart rate, sleep and HRV where your watch shares them, and answers from those numbers rather than from rules of thumb.

Ask it:
- what today's session should be, from how you feel and your recent runs;
- what your last run showed, compared with your usual;
- what time your goal race is likely to take, as a range with the odds of your goal;
- to plan your next two weeks around that race, then adjust after each run.

Every figure comes from your data, with how much data it rests on. Race times are always ranges, never a single promise. When you say you feel sore or run down, that outweighs good recovery numbers. Nothing in your plan is added, moved or deleted without your explicit yes in the chat, and the coach reads back what it changed.

What it keeps is yours: your profile and the notes you agree to save are listed on your account page, where you can edit or delete any of them. Pace Puffin trains no AI model on your data.

Before you connect: you make your Pace Puffin account the first time you connect. Add the coach, then sign in with your email address and the six-digit code we email you; there is no password. Then upload your watch's history export (Garmin, COROS, Suunto or Strava) on your account page, and the coach starts from that.

Pace Puffin is a training tool, not medical advice.

## Setup

Install the plugin, then connect its server (https://pacepuffin.com/mcp) when Claude asks: you sign in to your Pace Puffin account there. Then try:

- Give me today's check-in.
- How did my last run go?
- Plan my next week.
